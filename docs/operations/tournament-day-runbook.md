# Tournament-day runbook

Event: **Riftcore — 13 October 2026**

## T-60 minutes

- Open staff coordination channel.
- Confirm referees and lobby operators are present.
- Freeze last-minute ruleset changes.
- Verify registered teams and captain contact routes.
- Open player/team check-in.

## T-30 minutes

- Mark no-response teams for manual follow-up.
- Confirm bracket seeding.
- Publish first-round pairings.
- Assign staff responsibility per match/lobby.

## T-10 minutes

- Send lobby instructions to captains.
- Verify all starting rosters.
- Escalate substitutions or identity mismatches before draft begins.

## Match start

For every match, record:

1. Match ID.
2. Team A and Team B.
3. Lobby operator/referee.
4. Scheduled and actual start time.
5. Game-by-game winner.
6. Final series score.
7. Evidence/screenshots if required.

## Result confirmation

A reported result is not final until:

- both teams have had a reasonable opportunity to challenge it;
- the referee has resolved any dispute;
- the match state is marked `final`.

## Incident priority

1. Player safety or harassment.
2. Account/identity or eligibility dispute.
3. Lobby failure or server disruption.
4. Rule violation.
5. Score/reporting mismatch.
6. Delay/no-show.

Every ruling should be written down. Avoid relying on staff memory during later disputes.
